Custody

Your wallet. Your funds.

Peak is an interface, not a custodian. It has no account of its own and never takes ownership of your money. You trade from a wallet you control — sign in with an embedded wallet and Peak never sees your key, or import an existing Polymarket wallet and Peak signs on your instruction.

  • 01 Sign in without handing over a key.Embedded wallets via Privy — Peak never receives your private key.
  • 02 See the price before you sign.Outcome, bid, ask, and spread are on screen when you order.
  • 03 Funds stay in your wallet.Peak never takes ownership. Importing a key is optional — what that means.
